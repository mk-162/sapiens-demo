# Sapiens Terminology Audit

This audit compares terminology used in the demo against public Sapiens business/product terminology found on `sapiens.com` during the build review.

## Public reference points observed

The public Sapiens site uses the following visible positioning and product language:

- **Positioning:** intelligent insurance technology software solutions; AI-powered insurance platform; agile intelligence; faster time-to-value; optimised decision-making; personalised experiences.
- **Platform language:** Sapiens Insurance Platform; embedded agentic AI; last-inch functionality; outcomes across the insurance lifecycle.
- **Business lines:** Property & Casualty; Life & Pensions; Reinsurance.
- **Product / offering terms visible in navigation/footer:** IDITSuite for Property & Casualty; CoreSuite for Life & Pensions; DigitalSuite; DataSuite; Decision Management; Reinsurance; Underwriting P&C; Platform for Property & Casualty; Platform for Life & Pensions; Cloud Services.
- **Company proof points visible publicly:** 600+ customers, 38 countries, 5,265 experts.

## Summary verdict

The demo is **not pure dummy content**. It uses several real Sapiens/publicly supported terms as anchors, but wraps them in an **illustrative subscription packaging and pricing model** created for the demo.

Use this rule in stakeholder conversations:

- **Real:** public Sapiens products/business lines/brand language.
- **Derived:** how the demo groups those products into blocks/cohorts/packages.
- **Illustrative:** pricing, launch packages, quote output, June 30 status and fit-warning logic.

## Term-by-term audit

### Brand and positioning

- **Demo term:** “Advance the possible” / “Advancing the possible” style language
- **Status:** Derived / brand-aligned
- **Public support:** Sapiens public site uses outcome-led language around agile intelligence, faster time-to-value, optimised decision-making and personalised experiences. The phrase is consistent with the prior Sapiens brand profile used for this demo, but should be validated if presented as exact official copy.
- **Recommendation:** Fine for demo framing. If client-facing, confirm exact brand tagline from Sapiens brand guidelines.

### Sapiens Insurance Platform

- **Demo term:** Sapiens subscription portfolio / platform subscription
- **Status:** Derived / reasonable
- **Public support:** Public site uses “Sapiens Insurance Platform” and presents platform offerings across P&C, Life & Pensions and Reinsurance.
- **Recommendation:** Keep. Add caveat that “subscription portfolio” is the demo’s commercial packaging lens.

### Property & Casualty, Life & Pensions, Reinsurance

- **Demo term:** These appear as underlying business context/cohort targets.
- **Status:** Real / publicly supported
- **Public support:** These are visible Sapiens business lines.
- **Recommendation:** Safe terms.

### IDITSuite for Property & Casualty

- **Demo term:** Not directly represented as a module; the demo uses generic “Sapiens Horizon” and platform/core language.
- **Status:** Real public product, underused in demo
- **Public support:** Visible on public site as IDITSuite for Property & Casualty.
- **Recommendation:** If the demo is meant to mirror real product architecture, add IDITSuite explicitly or explain why it has been abstracted into the proposed subscription/package layer.

### CoreSuite for Life & Pensions

- **Demo term:** Not directly represented.
- **Status:** Real public product, missing/abstracted
- **Public support:** Visible on public site as CoreSuite for Life & Pensions.
- **Recommendation:** Consider adding a note that this demo currently focuses on a generic subscription packaging model and does not map every Sapiens core-system product.

### DigitalSuite

- **Demo term:** DigitalSuite Portals
- **Status:** Real anchor + derived module wording
- **Public support:** DigitalSuite is visible as a Sapiens offering.
- **Recommendation:** Keep, but validate exact product naming and capabilities before external use.

### DataSuite

- **Demo term:** DataSuite Real-Time Analytics
- **Status:** Real anchor + derived capability wording
- **Public support:** DataSuite is visible as a Sapiens offering.
- **Recommendation:** Keep as demo shorthand. Verify whether “Real-Time Analytics” is exact Sapiens copy before external use.

### Decision Management / Sapiens Decision

- **Demo term:** Decision & Intelligence; Sapiens Decision
- **Status:** Real anchor + derived grouping
- **Public support:** Decision Management is visible publicly. “Sapiens Decision” may be a known/legacy/product shorthand but should be source-checked if used externally.
- **Recommendation:** Rename UI copy to “Decision Management & Intelligence” if stronger public alignment is required.

### Underwriting P&C

- **Demo term:** AI Underwriting Copilot
- **Status:** Illustrative / not publicly verified as named product
- **Public support:** Underwriting P&C is visible as a Sapiens offering. The public site also uses AI/agentic AI positioning.
- **Recommendation:** Treat “AI Underwriting Copilot” as a concept/prototype unless validated. Safer external wording: “AI-assisted underwriting capability”.

### AI Claims Automation

- **Demo term:** AI Claims Automation
- **Status:** Illustrative / capability-level
- **Public support:** Sapiens publicly positions AI-powered insurance workflows, but this exact module/pricing construct is not verified from public navigation.
- **Recommendation:** Keep for internal demo only. Add caveat if shown to Sapiens: “illustrative module based on AI claims automation concept”.

### Cloud Services

- **Demo term:** Sapiens Cloud Hosting; Managed Cloud Operations; Premium AMS & Cloud Ops
- **Status:** Real anchor + derived commercial grouping
- **Public support:** Cloud Services is visible publicly.
- **Recommendation:** Keep as a demo grouping. Validate whether Sapiens uses AMS terminology publicly/internal-only.

### Reinsurance & Treaty Reporting

- **Demo term:** Reinsurance & Treaty Reporting
- **Status:** Real business line + derived module
- **Public support:** Reinsurance is visible publicly; treaty-reporting specifics are plausible but not validated from the visible public nav snapshot.
- **Recommendation:** Mark as illustrative module until validated with product SMEs.

### Sapiens Horizon

- **Demo term:** Sapiens Horizon
- **Status:** Illustrative / unverified package
- **Public support:** Not observed in public-site terminology captured during the audit.
- **Recommendation:** Treat as demo package name. If Sapiens has an internal Horizon initiative, cite that internal source; otherwise rename to “Evergreen Core Package” for safety.

### Sapiens Intelligent

- **Demo term:** Sapiens Intelligent
- **Status:** Illustrative / unverified package
- **Public support:** The public site uses “intelligent” and “agile intelligence” language, but this package name was not observed as a public product/package.
- **Recommendation:** Treat as demo package name. Safer alternative: “Decision, Data & Digital Package”.

### June 30 launch

- **Demo term:** June 30 launch
- **Status:** Illustrative / internal-context dependent
- **Public support:** Not publicly validated in this audit.
- **Recommendation:** Do not present as factual to Sapiens unless it came from an internal stakeholder brief. Keep as a scenario label.

### Future Platform / Maintain / End of Life

- **Demo term:** Lifecycle segmentation across portfolio/modules
- **Status:** Derived / demo operating model
- **Public support:** Not observed as public Sapiens terminology in the captured site content.
- **Recommendation:** Useful internal framing, but call it a portfolio segmentation model rather than official Sapiens taxonomy.

### Customer cohorts

- **Demo terms:** Tier 1 Enterprise; Tier 2–3 Mid-Market; MGAs & Specialty; Reinsurance & Global Specialty; Legacy On-Prem Holdouts
- **Status:** Derived / sales segmentation
- **Public support:** The industry segments are realistic and aligned to insurance markets, but these exact cohort labels were not validated from public Sapiens copy.
- **Recommendation:** Keep for sales strategy demo. Replace “Legacy On-Prem Holdouts” with softer external wording if client-facing, e.g. “Legacy Modernisation Candidates”.

### GWP and F_scale

- **Demo term:** GWP-based pricing scale factor
- **Status:** Illustrative pricing model
- **Public support:** GWP is a standard insurance metric; the formula itself is invented for the demo.
- **Recommendation:** Must be caveated in every quote/pricing surface.

### Surcharges and prices

- **Demo terms:** +15% Evergreen, +22% Decision, +18% Digital/Data, module base prices
- **Status:** Illustrative pricing model
- **Public support:** No public validation.
- **Recommendation:** Internal discussion only. Replace with approved price book before any real sales use.

## Practical demo caveat

Recommended wording for presenters:

> “The product and business-line language is grounded in public Sapiens terminology where possible. The package names, cohort model and pricing are illustrative so we can show how a subscription configurator could work. This is a sales-enablement prototype, not an official price book.”
