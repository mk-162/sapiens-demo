## Sales use

Use when a North American P&C carrier wants to improve claims speed, adjuster productivity and auditable automation.

## Discovery questions

- Which claims lines create the largest operational load?
- Where is the claims journey slowest: FNOL, triage, assignment, evidence review, payment or recovery?
- What automation can be straight-through versus human-supervised?
- Which audit, compliance and exception controls are mandatory?

## Proposal angles

- Lead with configurable claims automation, not uncontrolled autonomous settlement.
- Connect ClaimsPro to Decision for governed rules and DataSuite for claims visibility.
- Package AI as adjuster support and operational acceleration.

## Risks / validation notes

Keep AI claims conservative. Separate AI assistance, automation and final business decision rights. Validate regulated decisioning and claims governance with Sapiens product/legal teams.
