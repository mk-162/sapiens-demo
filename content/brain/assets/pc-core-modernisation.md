## Sales use

Use when a P&C carrier is carrying multiple legacy policy, billing or claims systems and needs a credible modernisation pathway. The sales motion should distinguish full-suite transformation from modular entry points.

## Discovery questions

- Which P&C lines are in scope first: personal, commercial, specialty or mixed portfolio?
- Is the primary blocker policy administration, billing, claims, distribution channels, or all of the above?
- Which region/product naming applies to this buyer?
- What is the current product-launch cycle time?
- Where are manual hand-offs creating operational risk?

## Proposal angles

- Position IDITSuite as the end-to-end P&C core for EMEA/APAC style opportunities.
- Position PolicyPro, BillingPro and ClaimsPro for North America-specific conversations.
- Attach DigitalSuite and DataSuite when the buyer needs a non-core transformation entry point.
- Use Decision where underwriting, product rules or claims automation are the bottleneck.

## Risks / validation notes

Do not imply one product family fits every region. Validate regional naming and product availability before external proposal use. Implementation timelines require current-state architecture discovery.
