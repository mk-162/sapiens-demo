## Sales use

Use when the buyer is not ready for full core replacement but needs better digital journeys, data visibility and business-controlled decision automation around existing systems.

## Discovery questions

- Which personas need better journeys: customers, agents, brokers, employers, administrators or claim handlers?
- Which data sources need to be consolidated for a 360-degree view?
- Which business rules change often and currently require IT release cycles?
- Where would decision automation have the highest business impact?

## Proposal angles

- Position DigitalSuite as the experience layer.
- Position DataSuite/DataHub as the operational data and AI-readiness layer.
- Position Decision where business users need faster control over rules and changes.

## Risks / validation notes

Do not present DataSuite as replacing every warehouse, actuarial model or enterprise BI platform without architecture discovery.
