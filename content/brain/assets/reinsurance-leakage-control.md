## Sales use

Use when buyers are managing treaty/facultative, ceded, assumed or retroceded reinsurance across spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

## Discovery questions

- Which reinsurance types are in scope: treaty, facultative, ceded, assumed or retroceded?
- Where does leakage occur: contract setup, bordereaux, calculations, claims, settlements or reporting?
- How many systems or spreadsheets hold treaty, premium, claims and recovery data?
- What financial controls and audit trails are required?

## Proposal angles

- Lead with control and recoverables visibility.
- Tie automation to leakage reduction, renewal speed and financial reporting confidence.
- Attach DataSuite for reporting and visibility across treaty/premium/claims data.

## Risks / validation notes

Validate regional product fit. Do not quantify leakage savings without customer data and Sapiens validation.
